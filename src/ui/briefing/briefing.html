Stage 1

The clock

Every stage is timed, and the clock starts the moment this closes. It does not stop while you think about it.

This one gives you ∞ — how long the stage actually takes people, and how long it takes you once you've cleared it a few times. Run it out with knots still on the board and that's the stage.

Except this one. Nobody has ever cleared it, so there's nothing to race and the corner reads ∞. What you do with it now is the number you'll be racing next time.

Cursed ropes

The black one is not a rope you move. Grabbing it costs ten, and anything still crossing it costs double to drag. Work the board around it.

It is also the biggest multiplier in the game. With a combo already running, pull a rope out of its field: everything banked gets multiplied, and the multiplier climbs with every further rope you haul off. Take it to ×10 and the rope detonates for free.

It cannot be started cold — build an ordinary combo on the clean ropes first, then turn it onto the black rope.

The window burns nearly twice as fast while cursed, and parking a rope back on the black one kills the run.
