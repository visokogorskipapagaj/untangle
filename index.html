THE CLOCK

Every stage has a clock on it, and it starts the moment this panel closes. No lead-in, no three, two, one — the readout in the corner is live from the second you can touch a rope, and it does not stop while you think about it.

This one gives you ∞. Nobody made that number up — it's how long the stage actually takes people, and once you've cleared it a few times it's how long it takes you. Run it down with knots still on the board and that's the stage.

Except this one. Nobody has ever cleared it, so there's nothing to measure you against and the corner reads ∞. Take as long as you like — what you do with it now is the number you'll be racing next time.

CURSED ROPES

That black one with the pulse in it is not a rope you move. Grabbing it costs ten, and anything still crossing it costs double to drag. Work the board around it and it gets cheaper as you clear ropes off it.

It is also the biggest multiplier in the game. Pull a rope out of its field while a combo is already running and the run goes cursed: everything the combo has banked gets multiplied, and the multiplier climbs with every further rope you haul off the black one.

So it is a two-part move, and that is the whole trick. Build an ordinary combo on the clean ropes first — a cursed combo cannot be started cold, because with no run going there is nothing to multiply — then turn it onto the black rope while the window is still open. Take it to ×10 and the rope detonates for free.

The window burns nearly twice as fast while it's cursed, and parking a rope back on the black one is a fresh knot — which kills the run and the multiplier with it.
